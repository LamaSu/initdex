Install initdex

  1. Plug the badge into this computer with a USB-C data cable (charge-only cables won't work).
  2. Hold PWR for about 2 seconds until the green LED lights, then release. The badge is now in download mode.
  3. Click Install initdex and pick the badge in the list (usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit").

Partition table read from your badge

    
The installer writes exactly two things: initdex into the spare app slot (app1, 0x310000) and the 8 KB boot selector (otadata, 0xe000). Your init() firmware (app0) and your profile and photo (ffat) are never written. It reads the badge's partition table first and stops without writing anything if the layout isn't the init() conference layout. Installing downloads the firmware from this site and the esptool-js flasher from unpkg.com.

    Privacy. While initdex runs, your badge continuously broadcasts the name, company and handle from your init() profile over ESP-NOW to other initdex badges within radio range; that is how the "nearby" radar works. A catch is saved to your Dex only when both people press KEY A within 6 seconds with badges close. Nothing is sent to any server: this page talks to your badge only over local USB, and the copy buttons only put text on your clipboard.
